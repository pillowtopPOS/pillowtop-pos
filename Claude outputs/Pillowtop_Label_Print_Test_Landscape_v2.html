Pillowtop landscape label print test (sample data only)

  1. Open this file on the warehouse Surface in Chrome or Edge. Press Ctrl+P and choose the thermal label printer.
  2. Paper size 4 x 6 in, margins None, scale 100%, headers and footers off, orientation Portrait.
  3. Print. You get two labels. Both are the same wide (landscape) design, turned two different ways: label 1 is turned clockwise, label 2 counter-clockwise.

This version draws 8 nested border lines (the outermost touches the very edge of the label). Each side of the printer may cut off some of them. For EACH label, hold it with the LONG side running left to right and count how many nested lines you can see on the TOP side, BOTTOM side, LEFT side and RIGHT side (8 means nothing cut off, 5 means the 3 outer lines are missing, and so on). Also tell me: which label reads right way up (1 or 2), does a scanner still read the barcode, printer model, and no blank trailing label.
