Pillowtop label print test (sample data only)

  1. Open this file on the warehouse Surface in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Press Ctrl+P. Choose the thermal label printer.
  3. Set paper size to 4 x 6 in (or 4x6 label), margins None, scale 100%, turn headers and footers off.
  4. Print. You should get two labels: a stock label and a customer label.

Check and tell me: (a) did both labels print as separate 4x6 labels with nothing cut off? (b) is the black border fully visible on all four sides and the A, B, C, D corners? (c) does a barcode scanner (or a phone scanner app) read 1042 from the stock label? (d) is the text sharp? (e) anything odd: blank label between them, wrong size, printer asked to pair or pick a port? A photo of both labels is perfect.
